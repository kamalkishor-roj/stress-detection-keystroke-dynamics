Stress Detection Using Natural Keystroke Dynamics

Welcome

Thank you for participating in this research study. This application collects anonymized keystroke timing data to analyze typing patterns under different stress conditions.

🔐 Privacy & Ethics Notice:
  • We do NOT store or log actual characters or typed text
  • We do NOT store key values
  • Only anonymized timing and derived features are collected
  • No personal identifiers are required
  • No login or backend connection required
  • All processing happens locally on your device

This study involves two typing tasks that will take approximately 5-6 minutes to complete.
